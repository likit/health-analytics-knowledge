# Small Change

“Small” is a descriptive judgment, not a substitute for a prespecified practical or clinical importance threshold. Prefer reporting the exact magnitude first. If using “slightly” or “broadly similar,” ensure the direction remains clear and disclose uncertainty where available.

Do not infer statistical non-significance from a small point estimate, and do not infer practical unimportance without an appropriate, verified decision context.

> Verification required: Any domain-specific meaningful-change threshold must have a source, population, and use context.
