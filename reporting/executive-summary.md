# Executive Summary

An executive summary should lead with the decision-relevant finding while preserving its evidence boundaries.

Include the population and time frame, exact direction and magnitude, denominator or cohort basis, material missingness or limitations, and uncertainty when available. Separate observed results from interpretation and recommended action. Avoid causal, clinical, or significance claims not established by the supplied analysis.
