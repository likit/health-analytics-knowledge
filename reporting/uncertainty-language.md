# Uncertainty Language

Match wording to the uncertainty information actually supplied.

- With an interval estimate, report the estimate and interval together and identify the interval type and level.
- Without an uncertainty analysis, say that statistical uncertainty was not assessed; do not substitute “significant” or “not significant.”
- Describe limitations specifically, such as missing data, small denominators, measurement differences, selection, or unmatched samples.
- Use “may,” “suggests,” or “is consistent with” only when they accurately express the supported degree of uncertainty; these words do not repair an otherwise unsupported claim.
