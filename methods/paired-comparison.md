# Paired Comparison

A paired comparison uses observations linked to the same person or other analytical unit at two or more time points.

Document the matching key, time-point definitions, duplicate-resolution rule, and handling of units missing one observation. Analyze the matched cohort when making individual-level change claims. Do not describe independent cross-sectional samples as paired merely because they come from the same population.

The appropriate statistical test depends on the outcome type, distribution, design, and assumptions; this draft does not prescribe one.
