# Knowledge Base Index

Use this page to navigate the repository. Content is illustrative and draft until its sources and domain review are recorded.

- [Concepts](concepts/index.md) define the meaning and boundaries of health measures and terms.
- [Indicators](indicators/index.md) specify derived quantities, their numerators, denominators, and interpretation limits.
- [Methods](methods/index.md) describe analytical procedures and calculations.
- [Interpretation](interpretation/index.md) constrains conclusions that may be drawn from evidence.
- [Reporting](reporting/index.md) guides evidence-aligned communication.
- [Templates](templates/) provide consistent starting points for new objects.
- [Golden cases](tests/golden-cases/README.md) document future regression tests for analyst behavior.

Repository purpose and architecture are described in the [README](README.md).
