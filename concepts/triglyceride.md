# Triglyceride

Triglyceride is a laboratory lipid measure. Its analytical use requires the reported unit, specimen and measurement context, and any applicable fasting context.

## Analytical semantics

- Retain units and do not compare unharmonized measurements.
- State the classification rule before labeling observations “normal” or “abnormal.”
- Distinguish a measured value from a clinical interpretation or diagnosis.
