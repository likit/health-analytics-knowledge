# Body Mass Index

Body mass index (BMI) is calculated as body weight in kilograms divided by height in metres squared.

## Analytical semantics

- Record the measurement units and the rule used to select height and weight observations.
- A category such as “abnormal” depends on an explicitly declared classification rule, population, and jurisdiction.
- BMI alone does not establish a diagnosis, causal explanation, or health outcome.
