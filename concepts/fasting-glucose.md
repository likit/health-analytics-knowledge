# Fasting Glucose

Fasting glucose is a blood glucose measurement for which the fasting context should be recorded or explicitly treated as unknown.

## Analytical semantics

- Preserve the reported unit and measurement method.
- Do not combine values expressed in different units without a documented conversion.
- “Abnormal” requires a declared, verified classification rule appropriate to the population and use case.
- Missing fasting status is not equivalent to confirmed fasting status.

> Verification required: Add authoritative definitions, pre-analytical requirements, and classification rules before clinical use.
